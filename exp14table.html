Types of Social Media Channels Example Princple
Vedio sharing platform Youtube "YT principle" likely refers to YouTube's core guidelines for content, especially for younger users, emphasizing safety, age-appropriateness, and positive experiences, balancing creator expression with protecting kids from harm like hate speech or predatory behavior.
Communication channel Instagram Instagram's core principles revolve around connecting people through shared moments (present tense focus), user experience (simplicity, ease of use, instant feedback), and algorithmic engagement (relevance through data, popularity, activity)
Digital channel Twitter Twitter (now X) operates on principles of real-time information sharing, freedom of expression, building healthy conversations, user trust, and platform openness, alongside technical principles like Separation of Concerns for scalability, all guided by strict rules against abuse, harassment, and sharing private info
Communication channel Snapchat Principles and Priorities Guiding Principles: Participation & Collaboration. Accountability. Non-discrimination, Equality & Intersectionality. Empowerment. Legality.
Broadcast Channels Facebook Facebook (now Meta) operates on core principles like being bold, focusing on impact, moving fast, being open, and building social value, aiming to connect people and foster community, alongside specific guidelines for privacy (user control, transparency) and advertising (don't sell data, be clear, safe)
