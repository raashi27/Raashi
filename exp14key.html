YOUTUBE

Key Aspects
  • Content Variety: From music videos and vlogs to gaming streams, tutorials, and documentaries, its content is incredibly diverse.
  • Creator Economy: It allows creators to earn revenue, building businesses and careers by sharing advertising and subscription income, notes YouTube.
  • Community & Interaction: Users can like, dislike, comment, and subscribe, fostering large online communities.
  • Accessibility: It's available globally in many languages and accessible on various devices, with TV now being a primary viewing platform in the US.
  • Evolution: Started with a dating site concept, it quickly became an open platform for all videos, growing exponentially through viral sharing and user uploads.

INSTAGRAM

Key Aspects
  • Posts: Share photos, videos (including carousels), add filters, tags, and locations; view in followers' feeds.
  • Stories: Full-screen, temporary (24hr) photo/video posts with text, stickers, music, polls, "Add Yours"..
  • Reels: Short, entertaining videos (like TikTok) with music, effects, and editing tools..
  • Live: Real-time video streaming to followers. .
  • Direct Messages (DMs): Private chats for sharing media, messages, and quick replies..
  • Explore Page: Discover new content and creators based on your interests..
  • Hashtags & Tags: Categorize content and find related posts..

TWITTER

Key Aspects
  • Tweets: Short posts (formerly 140, now often longer for premium users) with text, links, images, GIFs, or videos, answering "What's happening?".
  • Following: Subscribe to other users' updates to see them in your timeline (feed).
  • Hashtags : Use '#' to categorize tweets by topic, making them discoverable (e.g., #News, #Tech).
  • Mentions: Use '@' to tag other users in your tweet (e.g., @XSupport), notifying them.
  • Retweets : Share another user's tweet with your followers; can be simple or "Quote Tweets" with added commentary.
  • Likes : Show appreciation for a tweet, similar to favoriting.
  • Direct Messages: Private messages between users.

SNAPCHAT

Key Aspects
  • Snaps & Stories: Photos/videos (Snaps) disappear after viewing, while Stories are collections viewable for 24 hours, offering quick, real-time sharing.
  • Ephemeral Messaging: Direct chats delete by default after viewing, reducing pressure and promoting natural conversations.
  • Lenses & Filters: Advanced AR effects transform faces and surroundings, making content fun and interactive.
  • Memories: A personal archive to save favorite Snaps and Stories within the app, separate from ephemeral content.

FACEBOOK

Key Aspects
  • Personal Profiles & Timeline: Share info, photos, updates, and see friends' activities.
  • News Feed: Algorithm-driven stream of friends' posts, news, and media.
  • Messenger: Instant messaging, video/voice calls, and group chats.
  • Content Sharing: Text, photos, videos, Stories, and Facebook Live streaming.
  • Groups & Pages: Communities for shared interests & brand/public figure presence.
  • Marketplace: Buy and sell local goods.
  • Events: Organize and promote gatherings (virtual or in-person).
